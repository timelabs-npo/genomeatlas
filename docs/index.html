      Observation records stay separate from probe receipts. They can carry `probeTimestamp: null`, a distinct `recordedAt`, and always remain `UNVERIFIED` until an external reviewer links a real retained receipt.

      Probe receipts

      Imported and local receipts stay unverified until an external reviewer confirms them. Measured hashes must be 64 hexadecimal digits with an optional `sha256:` prefix, and the app never maps FAILED or UNKNOWN to not-detected.
